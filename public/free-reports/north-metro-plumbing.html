Overview

Here's the situation.

I looked at your site the way a potential customer would — on a phone, cold, no prior knowledge of the business.

North Metro Plumbing has a real business with real customers and a site that's been up long enough to have some history. Mobile-friendly, decent on desktop — those are genuine wins.

But the honest picture: you're ranking 28th on Google Maps for "Plumber + Ham Lake." Organic search traffic is estimated at one visitor per month. Listings across the web are inconsistent — some show a different company's name entirely at your address. And while the site is functional, it's not built to convert the people who do find it.

None of this is unfixable. The path forward is specific, not vague.

Google's baseline requirement — covered.
Fix This No clear value statement above the fold

Within 3 seconds it's not clear why someone should call you over the 27 businesses ranking above you. The site announces you exist — it doesn't sell you.

Worth Noting Phone number is clickable — but easy to miss

The number does dial on tap, which is good. But right now it's small underlined text that blends into the page. It should be a prominent, can't-miss button — something like a bold "Call Now" bar near the top of every page. The functionality is there; the visibility isn't.

Worth Noting No social links on the homepage

Your Facebook page exists (83 likes) but isn't linked from your site. Both Benjamin Franklin Plumbing and Roto-Rooter have this.

02 — Speed & Technical

How fast does it load?

Speed is a conversion issue. Slow sites lose visitors before they ever see your services.

Fix This Largest content element (LCP) is 4.2s on mobile

Google flags anything over 2.5s as "slow" and uses it as a search ranking signal. Your main content takes 4.2s to appear on mobile — this is actively hurting your ranking.

Worth Noting Page size is 2,157KB — images aren't optimized

Uncompressed images are a major contributor to the slow mobile time. Optimization alone would cut 30–40% off load time.

Working Well Interactivity (70ms) and visual stability are both excellent

Fast tap response, no layout shifts. Two of three Core Web Vitals are green.

This is a hosting problem, not just a content problem. Your site is on WordPress hosted on GoDaddy shared hosting — you're sharing server resources with hundreds of other sites. A site on modern edge infrastructure (the same network used by Netflix, Shopify, and high-traffic platforms) would load in under a second on any device, anywhere — automatically served from the nearest server to your visitor.

04 — Lead Capture

Is the site converting visitors?

Getting someone to the site is half the job. The other half is turning that visit into a call.

Worth Noting Phone number is clickable — but not prominent enough

It dials on tap, which is the right foundation. But small underlined text isn't a call to action. A bold sticky "Call Now" button visible at all times on mobile would convert significantly more visitors than a phone number buried in the header.

Worth Noting No emergency availability mentioned

Do you take after-hours calls? If so, say so prominently. That's what converts a panicked homeowner at 11pm — not a services list.

Worth Noting Service area isn't stated clearly

Ham Lake, Andover, Anoka, Blaine — if you serve these areas, say so on the homepage. Someone from a neighboring city needs to see their city mentioned to feel confident you'll come out.
